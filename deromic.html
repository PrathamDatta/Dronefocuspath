DeroMiC

Not another ground station. The system that connects them all.

Many aircraft, many OEMs, many locations: one coordinated operation.

DeroMiC · Indian Patent Application 202541104086 A

DeroMiC provides a unified operational framework for governing, planning, executing and overseeing UAV missions across multiple OEMs, organisations and locations, from the rules that govern a mission to the evidence it leaves behind.

Features

    Multi-OEM operationsPlan and oversee missions across integrated platforms from different makers.
  • 02
    Multi-UAV, multi-mission taskingAssign and track many missions without switching consoles.
  • 03
    Multi-location coordinationCoordinate teams and aircraft across distributed sites.
  • 04
