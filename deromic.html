Frequently asked questions.

What is DeroMiC?

DeroMiC is a patent-pending unified operational framework for governing, planning, executing and overseeing UAV missions across multiple OEMs, organisations and locations, from the rules that govern a mission to the evidence it leaves behind.

Is DeroMiC a ground station?

No. DeroMiC is not another ground station, fleet dashboard or UTM tool. It is the infrastructure underneath them, connecting aircraft and teams into one operating picture.

Does DeroMiC work with drones from different manufacturers?

Yes. DeroMiC supports multi-OEM operations: missions can be planned and overseen across integrated platforms from different makers, so new OEM aircraft can be added without rebuilding the operation.

What happens if the network link drops in the field?

DeroMiC works at the edge: missions continue in the field when links degrade, and mission evidence flows back for live view, replay and audit.

How can I see DeroMiC in action?

Derospace is open to demonstration requests from government, PSU, defence and critical-infrastructure operators. Use the contact page to request a demo.
