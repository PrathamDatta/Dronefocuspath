The Golden Minutes

The first minutes after an incident are the most valuable, in defence and civil operations alike. Aerial eyes that arrive in the Golden Minutes change the outcome; aerial eyes that arrive later mostly record it.

Illustrative concept chart, not measured data.

01Terror attacks & hostage situationsCommanders see the scene before teams move in.
02Natural disastersAfter an earthquake, flood or landslide, survivors can still be located and safe routes found.
03Search and rescueA missing person is still close by.
04Border & perimeter securityAn intruder is still near the fence.
05Fires & industrial accidentsA fire, gas leak or chemical spill can still be contained.
06Critical infrastructure & eventsA threat at a plant, pipeline, crowd or VIP route is checked before it escalates.
07Road & public safetyResponders know what they face before they arrive.
dXcale is built to be airborne inside the Golden Minutes.

The challenge with conventional drones

  • Packed in bulky hard cases that need two people, and often a vehicle, to move.
  • Lengthy unpacking, assembly and pre-flight setup before the aircraft can fly.
  • Hard to carry on foot into hills, forests, border posts or disaster zones.
  • By the time the drone is airborne, the moment that mattered has often passed.

Carry. Deploy. Fly. See. Act.

dXcale is a complete UAV system in one field backpack that one operator carries, airborne while the moment still matters.
