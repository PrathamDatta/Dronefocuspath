Frequently asked questions.

Is Dronocation DGCA-authorised?

Yes. Dronocation by Derospace Systems is a DGCA-authorised Remote Pilot Training Organisation, offering DGCA-approved remote pilot certification.

What does the training include?

Remote pilot certification includes a DGCA-approved syllabus, classroom, simulator and practical flying, certified instructors, assessment and certification support, and 12 months of access to Derospace's digital learning platform.

Can organisations train whole teams?

Yes. Institutional Training provides custom programmes for police, armed forces, utilities, PSUs and enterprises, on-site or at the Derospace training facility, with group certification and SOP and safety-procedure training.

What happens after the course ends?

Every participant gets 12 months of access to the digital learning platform, with lessons, videos, regulations and practice material available anytime. Operational Capability Development adds drills, readiness assessment and recurrent training.
