Frequently asked questions.

What is dXcale?

dXcale is a patent-pending rapid-deployment UAV system from Derospace Systems. A complete UAV system packs into one field backpack that a single operator can carry, so the aircraft can be airborne while the moment still matters.

What are the Golden Minutes?

The Golden Minutes are the first minutes after an incident, which are the most valuable in defence and civil operations alike, for example after terror attacks, natural disasters, search and rescue, border incidents, fires and public-safety emergencies. dXcale is built to be airborne inside them.

How many people are needed to carry dXcale?

One. The complete system fits in a single field backpack, versus a two-person carry for conventional drone hard cases. Four dXcale systems fit in the footprint of one conventional drone case.

Can dXcale work with DeroMiC?

Yes. dXcale is a fully independent system that also joins coordinated DeroMiC missions when more than one aircraft is needed.

What are the dXcale specifications?

Derospace publishes figures from measured tests only, and the dXcale specification values are currently to be confirmed. Ruggedisation is underway and field trials are open for early partners.
